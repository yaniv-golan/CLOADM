WYRMFIRE, served by CLOADM

(https://freedoom.github.io), Copyright (c) 2001-2024 Contributors to the Freedoom project, under the BSD
3-Clause licence in freedoom/COPYING.txt. The contributors are listed in freedoom/CREDITS.txt and
freedoom/CREDITS-MUSIC.txt. CLOADM's tools/wyrmfire-vendor.js rebuilds it: it downloads the Freedoom 0.13.0
release at its pinned SHA-256 and converts it with the converter above.
