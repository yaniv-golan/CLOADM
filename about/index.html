About CLOADM

Made by someone who had a Dragon as a kid. My day job is in venture capital; this has nothing to do with it. — Yaniv

What it is

CLOADM runs the Dragon 32, 64 and 200, the Tandy Color Computer 1, 2 and 3, the MC-10 and their clones in a web page. There is nothing to install, no account and no ROMs included. Without ROMs it still runs the welcome menu, LIZA, the games and firmware written for this project; add your own ROM dumps and you have BASIC. Your ROMs and files stay in your browser. It works offline once visited.

I check it against MAME, the reference emulator, instruction by instruction and pixel by pixel, wherever MAME has the hardware. How it is verified lists the results and the exceptions. Talking to Claude is optional, and can be switched off in Settings. Questions are answered in the FAQ.
