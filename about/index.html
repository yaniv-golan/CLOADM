About CLOADM

Made by someone who had a Dragon as a kid. My day job is in venture capital; this has nothing to do with it.

What it is

CLOADM runs the Dragon 32, 64 and 200, the Tandy Color Computer 1, 2 and 3, the MC-10 and their clones in a browser, checked against MAME instruction by instruction. It is free and open source under the MIT License, with no account and no ROMs included. Questions are answered in the FAQ.

Credits and thanks

  • MAME, our reference: every instruction and every pixel is compared against MAME 0.264.
  • XRoar by Ciaran Anscomb, our benchmark and second opinion.
  • VCC, the Virtual Color Computer, and the VCCE community.
  • Freedoom, whose level ships with WYRMFIRE.
