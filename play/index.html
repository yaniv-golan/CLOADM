python3 -m http.server 8000
npx serve -l 8000 .

Translated: keys type what they say. Positional: the key's position on your keyboard is
        used, so an AZERTY keyboard reaches the letters printed on the machine.
        Esc BREAK · Home CLEAR · Backspace ← · arrows · Enter ENTER.
        Pasted text types itself in.
